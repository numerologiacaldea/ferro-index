The rule

Only excellence gets named here

A hotel enters the Register only when a real guest measures it above 85 with the Ferro Index, submits it with name and reasoning, and the nomination passes a human check. It cannot be bought, it cannot be requested: it has to be earned.

Scores under 85 never appear with a name. The Register exists to point at true luxury, not to expose whoever falls short: that part stays anonymous, and becomes statistics.

The band is rare by design, as in the professional systems, where the top threshold is reached only by holding everything together at once. A short Register is a Register that means something.
