The rule

Only excellence gets named here

A hotel enters the Register only when a real guest measures it above 85 with the Ferro Index, submits it with name and reasoning, and the nomination passes a human check. It cannot be bought, it cannot be requested: it has to be earned.

Scores under 85 never appear with a name. The Register exists to point at true luxury, not to expose whoever falls short: that part stays anonymous, and becomes statistics.

The band is rare by design, as in the professional systems, where the top threshold is reached only by holding everything together at once. A short Register is a Register that means something.

The sanctuaries

The first verdicts are being read

The Register opens with the first accepted nominations. If you've stayed at a hotel that deserves a place here, measure it: if it clears 85, you can submit it from the verdict page.

The Sanctuary Register is part of the Ferro Index, a method by Mattia Ferro. No property pays to be measured or published, no review enters the score. The score is declared by the guest who lived the stay: I send no inspectors and I do not check the facts they report. What I do is read every nomination and decide whether to publish it.

Mattia Ferro is an independent travel advisor of Fora Travel, New York. Bookings never touch the score.
